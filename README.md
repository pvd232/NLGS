# Natural Language Generation and Summarization

Peter Driscoll’s Fall 2026 research workspace for Columbia’s COMS 6975. The project asks whether an encoder can learn what harm means from Spanish testimony in the Casanare hearings of Colombia’s Special Jurisdiction for Peace (JEP). People may describe the same event and different consequences for their families. Retrieval and clustering let the research team inspect those distinctions in the original passages.

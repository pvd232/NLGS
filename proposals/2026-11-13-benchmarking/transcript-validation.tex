\section{Introduction and Objectives}
The JEP-03 Casanare proceedings comprise extensive audiovisual recordings of hearings before the Special Jurisdiction for Peace (JEP). To support downstream legal analysis, entity extraction, and representation learning, speech-to-text (STT) transcripts must undergo rigorous quality assurance. This protocol defines the sampling methodology, error-rate estimation, and audit thresholds for validating automated transcripts against the originating hearing recordings.

\section{Validation Methodology}
\subsection{Sampling Frame.}
Transcripts will be audited using human review across 60 randomly selected, non-overlapping one-minute intervals drawn uniformly from the corpus of JEP-03 hearing video recordings. The stratified audit covers both continuous speech segments and interval boundaries to inspect potential alignment drift or recording dropouts.

\subsection{Error-Rate Estimation and Confidence Bounds.}
For each audited window, human reviewers will transcribe the audio verbatim and document word error rates (WER) and character error rates (CER) against the automated transcript. 

Given the sample of $n = 60$ intervals, the estimated transcription error rate $\hat{p}$ will be documented with two-sided 95\% confidence bounds ($\alpha = 0.05$) using the Wilson score interval:
\[
CI_{0.95} = \frac{\hat{p} + \frac{z^2}{2n} \pm z \sqrt{\frac{\hat{p}(1-\hat{p})}{n} + \frac{z^2}{4n^2}}}{1 + \frac{z^2}{n}}
\]
where $z = \Phi^{-1}(1 - \alpha/2) \approx 1.96$.

\section{Quality Control and Remediation Thresholds}
A materially high error rate (exceeding established tolerance for legal discourse) will trigger secondary remediation:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Acoustic \& STT Adaptation:} Fine-tuning specialized Colombian Spanish acoustic/speech-to-text models on verified hearing segments.
    \item \textbf{Active Learning Human Verification:} Margin-aware support vector machine (SVM) uncertainty sampling to identify low-confidence transcript spans for targeted expert review.
\end{enumerate}
